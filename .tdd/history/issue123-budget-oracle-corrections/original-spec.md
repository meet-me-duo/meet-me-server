## Public boundary to agree before skeleton/RED

- PostgreSQL is the authority for one lifelong budget scope per room. All physical AI requests, including count requests and requests that fail/refuse/time out, belong to that scope. Participant/account/revision/run/invocation/correction/replay changes do not mint a new budget.
- Synthetic tests use `B=100` arbitrary integer test subunits with artificial prices/evidence only. They do not choose KRW/USD amount, FX, a production profile or actual provider cost. Conservative checked arithmetic enforces `confirmed spent + unresolved maximum holds + new quote < B`, not `<=`. No binary floating-point money, wraparound or downward rounding.
- Immutable server-validated quote binds room/execution identity, purpose, phase/physical ordinal, complete payload/context hash, provider/API/model/version/profile, upper billed quantities, input/output/reasoning/count/access price components, mandatory multipliers, currency/FX/price/evidence versions and validity interval. A boolean `verified=true` or a caller-provided fingerprint/amount alone cannot grant dispatch.
- Separate successful reservation persistence from authority to execute HTTP. A committed one-time dispatch claim/CAS is required immediately before exactly one physical call. The ledger itself provides no supplier exactly-once guarantee after dispatch intent and crash. No automatic resend/refund on lease expiration.
- Known token usage, complete usage metadata, synthetic evidence and an actual proven bill are distinct. No observation or `billedCostKnown=true` flag alone can release a hold. Financial settlement needs trusted evidence matching the immutable reservation and independently vetted price/cap profile.
- Product ownership/revision/generation/room-close/deadline fencing remains authoritative. Financial observations can arrive late and settle their own call without publishing any product result. Budget admission never supplies product ownership authority or extends a deadline.
- Default OFF/default DENY remains effective with keys present, missing policy or unproven caps/prices. No new user approval interface, policy amount, exhausted-budget UX or live profile is introduced by this design.

### Existing public-port gaps (not implementation choices)

`AiCallBudgetRequest` currently has room/execution/generation/stage/purpose/hashes/maximumPhysicalCalls; `AiCallQuote` has only profile/evidence fingerprints, call bound and a boolean. `reserve()` returns an opaque reservation whose dispatchGranted flag can be represented by a caller-created data class; `record()` accepts safe token observations but no proven monetary amount. This is the earlier default-deny/fake stage contract, not a durable money/dispatch protocol.

Before tests compile, agree additive/replacement signatures for: immutable monetary quote authority; persistent per-physical-call identity; dispatch claim with a bound single-use token; unknown versus proven no-dispatch cancellation; trusted financial evidence/idempotent reconciliation; read-only budget snapshot. SQL/table/class names remain implementer choices. Tests call public application/adapter contracts, never private SQL helpers or implementation-derived constants.

A stage bundle with maximumPhysicalCalls2 does not prove two independent physical reservations. Preferred scope: count and generation each have a quoted child call identity/hold/lease under the same operation. Whether a parent bundle hold preallocates child maxima is internal, provided no double-counted hold and every child dispatch is independently authorized. Future port stage/purpose coverage must include classification/count/generation/final-primary/fallback and the existing HOST/correction/DLQ causes without creating fresh room scopes.

## Decisions/blockers to send to root

1. Port/state contract: per-physical identities/claims and immutable money/proof snapshots must be expressible; current opaque quote/bool+two stages cannot implement durable all-path accounting alone. Choose public signatures and lock ownership before independent RED; do not pick SQL/table names as test expectations.
2. Real budget/currency/FX/tax/price/cap evidence and approved profiles are missing. This blocks live grant/activation, not synthetic durable TDD/default-deny preparation. Preserve PRD10KRW requirement as a requirement, without claiming it is enforced for live requests by synthetic figures.
3. Historical bootstrap and financial retention/FK policy need a conservative root agreement. Proposed default is deny historical uncertainty and retain unresolved exposure; no spent0 backfill, deletion-based reset or invented retention period.
4. Trusted completed-bill/partial-component/conflicting-evidence authority needs a typed contract. Known usage alone is insufficient; arbitrary booleans or a review-looking URL cannot prove an invoice or release holds. Proposed initial safe implementation settles complete physical-call proofs only, separately records partial usage, and quarantines conflicts.
5. Exhaustion/UNKNOWN product UX, protected final-analysis allotment, operator reconciliation rights and any participant/deadline change remain outside agent discretion. Their absence must not make budget denial CLEAR/completed or extend a room deadline. Internal deny/preserve-original behavior can be prepared safely; a newly exposed API status/text requires separate agreement.
